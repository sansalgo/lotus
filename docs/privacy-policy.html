Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Lotus is a habit tracking app built by sansalgo. This page explains what data the app collects and how it is handled.

Data We Collect

Lotus collects no personal data. All habit data you create (habit names, goals, completion records) is stored locally on your device using a SQLite database. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server.

Data Shared With Third Parties

We do not share any data with third parties. Lotus has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no network requests.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Lotus schedules local notifications on your device. Notification preferences are stored locally and are not shared with anyone.

Data Deletion

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all habit data stored on your device. There is no account or server-side data to delete.

Children's Privacy

Lotus does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
